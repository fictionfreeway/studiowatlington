To commemorate 40 years of service, Precision Electronic Services needed a fresh anniversary logo and supporting visuals for their website, email signatures, and customer newsletter. The design aimed to celebrate this milestone while maintaining a professional, brand-consistent aesthetic.

Studio Watlington delivered a bold yet timeless anniversary mark, along with custom digital assets to enhance their online and email presence. Created using Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop, these designs reinforced the company's legacy while ensuring a cohesive look across multiple platforms.
