Designs that Shape Your Community.

The Blue Ridge Regional Library (BRRL) serves tens of thousands of residents across Martinsville, Henry County, and Pittsylvania County, Virginia. With such a wide-reaching presence, clear communication and a consistent visual identity are vital. Before its redesign, BRRL’s web presence felt outdated, and its design lacked cohesion—making it harder for patrons to connect meaningfully with the library’s services.

William Watlington led a full-scale visual transformation for BRRL, developing a fresh, unified identity that extends across print, web, and social media. This included a complete website overhaul focused on usability and accessibility, a sleek redesign of the library card, and a full suite of branded materials—from event banners to social media graphics. Every piece was designed to reinforce BRRL’s modern, professional, and approachable image across all touchpoints.

But this wasn't just a visual facelift—it was a strategic move toward deeper public engagement and long-term growth. The new branding enhances user trust, simplifies access to resources, and sets the stage for years of consistent, compelling outreach across the region’s three counties.

This project highlights what powerful, intentional design can do for a community institution. And it reflects Studio Watlington’s core belief: great branding builds lasting connections—whether you’re a library, a business, or anything in between.
