Designs that Shape Your Community.

The Blue Ridge Regional Library (BRRL) serves a broad community across Martinsville, Henry County, and Pittsylvania County, Virginia, connecting tens of thousands of residents to resources, programs, and educational opportunities. With such a widespread presence, a strong and consistent visual identity is essential to ensure clear communication and engagement with the public. However, before its transformation, BRRL struggled with an outdated web presence and inconsistent graphic design, making it difficult to effectively connect with its patrons.

William Watlington led a complete overhaul of BRRL’s branding, bringing a modern, unified look across all platforms. This included a full website redesign to replace an outdated and difficult-to-navigate online experience, the creation of a sleek and professional new library card, and the development of cohesive social media graphics and promotional materials. These improvements established a clear and engaging identity for BRRL, ensuring that patrons across all three counties could easily recognize and interact with library services, whether online, in print, or in person.

This transformation is more than just a fresh coat of paint—it is a long-term investment in BRRL’s future. The new branding strengthens public engagement, enhances accessibility, and creates a polished and professional presence that will serve the library and its patrons for years to come. With a reach spanning three counties and thousands of residents, this visual identity ensures that BRRL stands out as a modern, welcoming institution.

This project is a testament to the power of effective branding in revitalizing an organization’s presence. The same principles of clarity, engagement, and longevity guide Studio Watlington’s approach, helping businesses and organizations build a strong, lasting connection with their audiences.
